wasthon — CPython test_struct.py vs wasthon _struct

Runs CPython's own Lib/test/test_struct.py against the wasthon _struct module (via the stdlib struct.py wrapper). Proof-of-concept: behavioural ground truth from CPython's suite, far more thorough than the hand-written probes. @cpython_only and subprocess tests are stubbed to skip — they're environment limits, not wasthon bugs.
